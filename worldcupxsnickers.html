World Cup × Snickers

A university project where I was tasked with creating a promotional campaign using social media platforms.
I chose TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Campaign purpose was to show how the world cup brings people together, using the opportunity
to highlight people other than the players who make the World Cup what it is e.g. the fans, linesmen or referees.

Shot using iPhones and Sony FX30 camera


TikToks

3 TikToks where I focused on using trending sounds and challenges and video formats to create engaging posts.
These were edited by me using DaVinci Resolve.

YouTube Videos

2 Longer 'hero' skit advertisements for a YouTube focused audience, where I exercised the
use of more cinematic techniques and storytelling to create engaging posts.
These were directed by me and edited using DaVinci Resolve.

Instagram Posts (10)

Photos of the products to be used as promotional images throughout the campaign.
These were taken by me and the 2 which I star in were directed by me.
